## Remaining geometric steps

Proposition 4.21 still has hypotheses at the point where the two geometric proofs meet the semiring presentation. In `TensorComparison.lean`, the declaration `GeneralizedEuler.Section4.tensorIsoOfCharts` proves the isomorphism conditionally. Its inputs include a finite rectangle assignment to every graded RV object, invariance under each defining scissor relation, coverage, and recovery of pure tensors. Lean proves both inverse identities from these inputs.

To obtain the manuscript proposition for the actual categories, the remaining work includes constructing the fixed-base cell assignment for arbitrary RV formulas and bijections, realizing the positive shears as signed RV-reduct automorphisms, and assembling the chart and height comparisons into the scissor relations for the concrete pullback from the value category. The height proof also needs the full-dimensional independent point and the dimension drop for unmatched remainders. The ring computations in 4.22–4.24 can be applied to the geometric RV ring once the category identifications and the value-cell and Euler-characteristic inputs are supplied.
