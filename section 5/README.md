# Section 5 · Generalized Euler characteristic

Run `lake build` in this folder and import `Section5`. The package uses the same Lean toolchain and pinned Mathlib as Section 4.

| Manuscript | Lean coverage |
| --- | --- |
| 5.1–5.4 | `UnaryPreparation` and `FiniteCenters` construct regularization and the finite sequence of centripetal transformations. The labels retain every previous leading term. Their inverse fibers are open discs or singletons. `FixedBaseCenters`, `PreparationGraph`, and `UnaryDeformation` obtain one finite list of centers in the VF sort of the original base, define the graph over both sorts of that base, and prove the pullback assertion for the whole RV-parameter family. The model-theoretic inputs are saturation for the indicated tuple and the immediate-isomorphism criterion from Section 3. |
| 5.5, induction | `VerticalCenters` and `UniformVerticalPreparation` choose one finite list of center terms for all complementary field tuples and auxiliary RV values. `HeadPreparation` shows that the leading terms of the pairwise center differences determine the possible histories. `DescendedVerticalGraph` defines the graph after dropping the prepared field coordinate. `VerticalSuccessor` checks the image and injectivity of the combined head and tail maps. `FieldArityPreparation` assembles the induction in every finite field arity. The result gives a fixed-base definable graph and a special chain with explicit coordinates, foci and RV-hull conditions. `SpecialPrefixGraphs` gives the fixed-base graphs between the head prefixes. |
| 5.6 and 5.8 | `UnaryFunctions` prepares all RV outputs of a unary definable graph at once. `PreparedGraphContraction` proves pullback and contraction for the restriction to a graph in all arities. `FiniteFieldPreimages` proves that each prepared field tuple has finitely many original field tuples above it. `GradedLiftPresentation` presents the resulting object as the union of the padded lifts of fixed-base Section 4 objects in all arities. A component retains its nonzero leading terms as structure coordinates and pads the omitted field coordinates by zero. |
| 5.12 | `LiftDimensionInduction` proves the fixed-base definable lift in every ambient arity under the stated RV dimension laws, pointwise coordinate basis bound, Section 3 boundary estimate, saturation, and elementarity inputs. It uses the finite diagonal scaling case, definable gluing over the two RV boundaries, simultaneous source and target projection partitions, and fixed-base field choices and scalings for the omitted coordinates. |
| 5.13 | `RVObjectLiftTheorem` extends the fixed-base lifting result to isomorphisms of finite-to-one RV objects. It applies Section 4's tag partition to both structure maps, lifts the induced bijections on their label sets, and glues the mixed VF–RV graphs. |
| 5.15 | `UnaryAffinePreparation` prepares the field-branch tag and the derivative's leading term together, assuming a definable tag graph and the branchwise mean-value theorem. Every prepared nonpoint fiber has the asserted RV slope. The field graph and pullback presentation use the original base. |
| 5.17–5.25 | `BalancedCells` treats the zero paradigm as a graph, proves both translated-fiber formulas in the balanced case, and transfers the target radius inequality to the opposite paradigm. `RelativeUnaryShears` gives the three relatively unary transformations exchanging two field coordinates. The full two-cell decomposition and its comparison of contraction orders still need to be assembled. |
| 5.27 | `ZeroBlowupObject` and `PositiveBlowupObject` give the two graded RV objects of an elementary blowup. The multiplier set and both structure maps are definable over the fixed base, and the finite deletion bound gives finite fibers in each degree. |
| 5.29–5.33 | `GradedScissorRealization` extracts a fixed-base RV family isomorphism in every degree from equality of positive classes. `BlowupOverlap` gives the carrier equivalence for the two orders of overlapping loci, separately for zero and nonzero multipliers. `BlowupIteration` and `BlowupCongruence` verify the exact-length grid and the semiring congruence from the one-step diamond and transport laws. |
| 5.34–5.35 | `MultiCoordinateBlowup` verifies the lift equivalence for chosen foci, allowing a focus to depend on the unchanged field tuple. `BlowupFocusChoice` chooses such a focus definably when the retained RV projection is injective. |
| 5.39, elementary relation | `BlowupClassProducts` shows that each elementary class difference factors through the difference for the degree-one point. This checks the elementary ideal calculation. |

The general blowup comparison and the integration theorems are in progress.
