# Section 5 · Generalized Euler characteristic

Run `lake build` in this folder and import `Section5`. The package uses the same Lean toolchain and pinned Mathlib as Section 4.

| Manuscript | Lean coverage |
| --- | --- |
| 5.1–5.4 | `UnaryPreparation` and `FiniteCenters` construct regularization and the finite sequence of centripetal transformations. The labels retain every previous leading term. Their inverse fibers are open discs or singletons. `FixedBaseCenters`, `PreparationGraph`, and `UnaryDeformation` obtain one finite list of centers in the VF sort of the original base, define the graph over both sorts of that base, and prove the pullback assertion for the whole RV-parameter family. The model-theoretic inputs are saturation for the indicated tuple and the immediate-isomorphism criterion from Section 3. |
| 5.5, induction | `VerticalCenters` and `UniformVerticalPreparation` choose one finite list of center terms for all complementary field tuples and auxiliary RV values. `HeadPreparation` shows that the leading terms of the pairwise center differences determine the possible histories. `DescendedVerticalGraph` defines the graph after dropping the prepared field coordinate. `VerticalSuccessor` checks the image and injectivity of the combined head and tail maps. `FieldArityPreparation` assembles the induction in every finite field arity. The result gives a fixed-base definable graph and a special chain with explicit coordinates, foci and RV-hull conditions. `SpecialPrefixGraphs` gives the fixed-base graphs between the head prefixes. |
| 5.6 and 5.8 | `UnaryFunctions` prepares all RV outputs of a unary definable graph at once. `PreparedGraphContraction` proves pullback and contraction for the restriction to a graph in all arities. `FiniteFieldPreimages` proves that each prepared field tuple has finitely many original field tuples above it. `MixedLiftPresentation` starts with a definable mixed set with finite field fibers and obtains a special bijection to the union of padded fixed-base lifts. A component retains its nonzero leading terms as structure coordinates and pads the omitted field coordinates by zero. `RVObjectLifts` verifies finite field fibers for each lift. |
| 5.10 | `PairedMixedFocus` compares a paired focus step with arbitrary old RV tags under the disc-to-disc hypotheses. `PairedSourceTranslation` constructs the source translation from the inverse of the target focus and verifies its leading term. `PairedFocusDefinability` supplies the fixed-base graph. `SimultaneousLabelRefinement` gives the induction, and `DefinableContractionIso` extracts a fixed-base RV isomorphism from contractions in both directions. |
| 5.12 | `LiftDimensionInduction` proves the fixed-base definable lift in every ambient arity under the stated RV dimension laws, pointwise coordinate basis bound, Section 3 boundary estimate, saturation, and elementarity inputs. It uses the finite diagonal scaling case, definable gluing over the two RV boundaries, simultaneous source and target projection partitions, and fixed-base field choices and scalings for the omitted coordinates. |
| 5.13 | `RVObjectLiftTheorem` extends the fixed-base lifting result to isomorphisms of finite-to-one RV objects. It applies Section 4's tag partition to both structure maps, lifts the induced bijections on their label sets, and glues the mixed VF–RV graphs. |
| 5.14 | `FieldScissors` defines the positive VF scissor semiring from Section 4's filtered VF objects. `LiftClassHom` checks the lift respects all scissor relations. `CanonicalObjectLift` gives the isomorphism-lifting input from saturation and the label lifts. `FieldClassPreparation` proves surjectivity under the fixed-base preparation hypotheses by cutting into padded graded lifts. |
| 5.15 | `UnaryAffinePreparation` prepares the field-branch tag and the derivative's leading term together, assuming a definable tag graph and the branchwise mean-value theorem. Every prepared nonpoint fiber has the asserted RV slope. The field graph and pullback presentation use the original base. |
| 5.16–5.25 | `PositioningDistance` proves constant positioning distance on the inverse of a positioned disc and the product formula when the image lies in a sufficiently small target disc. `BalancedCells` treats the zero paradigm as a graph, proves both translated-fiber formulas in the balanced case, and transfers the target radius inequality to the opposite paradigm. `RelativeUnaryShears` gives the three relatively unary transformations exchanging two field coordinates. The full two-cell decomposition and its comparison of contraction orders still need to be assembled. |
| 5.27 | `ZeroBlowupObject` and `PositiveBlowupObject` give the two graded RV objects of an elementary blowup. The multiplier set and both structure maps are definable over the fixed base, and the finite deletion bound gives finite fibers in each degree. |
| 5.29–5.33 | `PositiveRVClasses` represents every positive class by finitely many actual objects. `GradedFamilyIsomorphism` and `GradedClassCuts` extract a degree-preserving, fixed-base bijection from class equality and cut both families along its graph. `ElementaryClassSteps` identifies one step with the class formula for an actual elementary blowup plus its untouched part. `ClassBlowupDiamond` proves the diamond and exact complementary lengths on these classes. `GradedBlowupCongruence` proves the semiring congruence and identifies its generator as the degree-one point blowup. |
| 5.34–5.35 | `FocusDeletionFiniteness` derives the deletion bound from relative finiteness of the focus image. `FocusTranslationImage` identifies the two strata of a full focus translation with the elementary blowup lifts. `DefinableElementaryLift` gives a fixed-base definable bijection for injective retained projection. `FiniteProjectionElementaryLift` partitions and glues the bijections for finite-to-one retained projections using saturation and generated-base elementarity. `ElementaryLiftStrata` gives the two output strata, including zero padding for the lower degree. |
| 5.36–5.39, comparison steps | `FiberwiseBlowupFiniteness` gives the finite deletion bound when the retained labels determine finitely many slice indices. `ContractionOrderInduction` reduces order independence to adjacent exchanges and carries comparisons along a relatively unary chain. The geometric adjacent exchanges and uniform descent are separate inputs to these steps. |
| 5.39, elementary relation | `BlowupLiftClass` proves equality of the actual VF scissor classes across elementary blowups under the fixed-base lift hypotheses. It checks the degree-one point relation and gives the induced quotient lift. The converse geometric kernel criterion is separate. |
| 5.40, quotient algebra | `GroupifiedBlowupQuotient` identifies the groupification of the common-blowup quotient with the Grothendieck ring modulo the elementary principal ideal using the two universal properties. Identifying this quotient with the VF ring still needs the converse geometric kernel criterion. |

The comparison along arbitrary special chains, the geometric kernel criterion, and the integration theorems are in progress.
